Get More From Your Coconut Oil

Simple information to help you understand, use and store your Aroga Foods Wood Pressed Coconut Oil.

🔎

Focus Points

Things to know when choosing and using coconut oil.

  • Check the product name, ingredients and label information.
  • Check the packaging seal before use.
  • Use clean and dry utensils when handling the oil.
  • Keep the bottle closed when not in use.
  • Always check the best-before date printed on the pack.
🍳

How to Use

Coconut oil can be used in everyday cooking and traditional personal-care routines.

  • Use for sautéing and everyday cooking.
  • Add to suitable recipes according to your preference.
  • It can be used in traditional hair-care routines.
  • It may also be used as a moisturizing oil in personal care.

⚠️

What Not To Do

Simple precautions can help maintain the quality of your oil.

  • Do not use a wet or dirty spoon inside the bottle.
  • Do not leave the bottle open for long periods.
  • Do not store it near direct sunlight or excessive heat.
  • Do not use the product after its best-before date.
  • Do not assume that consuming more oil means more health benefits.
🫙

Storage Guide

Proper storage helps maintain the quality of coconut oil.

  • Store in a cool and dry place.
  • Keep away from direct sunlight.
  • Keep the cap tightly closed.
  • Avoid introducing water or moisture into the bottle.
  • Follow the storage instructions printed on the product label.
